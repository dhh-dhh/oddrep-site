spduan@mail.ustc.edu.cn

写信时请附上：机型、系统版本、App 版本（设置页底部可见），以及问题出现的操作步骤。

没有 Apple Watch 能体验吗

可以。iPhone 端「历史记录」里点「查看示例记录」，会立刻载入四条示例，能完整体验详情页、三张过程曲线和 CSV 导出。示例记录带橙色标识，永远不会写入苹果健康，也不会出现在导出文件里。

次数明显偏多或偏少

次数来自手腕加速度峰值的估算。到设置里调这一项活动的「灵敏度」和「最短计数间隔」：数多了就先把间隔调大，数少了就调小。佩戴位置（腕骨上方一指）和表带松紧影响很大，换个姿势可以先用「演示模式」对比一下。

记录过程中断了

App 每 5 秒保存一次检查点，异常中断后重新打开会从最后一个检查点恢复。不保证无损，中断前最后几秒可能丢失。

手表记完，手机上没出现

确认两台设备蓝牙可用、手机 App 在前台。手表端会以 1 秒一次发送状态；已保存的记录文件会在重连后排队同步，不需要重新记录。控制类的请求（开始、暂停、结束）不会排队，断线期间的操作需要重做。

苹果健康里的训练重复了

关闭概览页的「自动写入苹果健康」，改为在记录详情里手动点击「同步到苹果健康」。已经写入的重复训练需要在「健康」App 里手动删除，奇动无法代为删除。

数据会传到服务器吗

不会。App 没有任何联网代码。所有记录只存在手表和 iPhone 本地。详见隐私政策。

Please include your device model, OS version, app version (shown at the bottom of Settings), and the steps that led to the problem.

Can I try it without an Apple Watch?

Yes. On iPhone, open Records and tap "View Sample Records". Four samples load instantly, and you can open each one to see the detail screen, the three synchronized charts, and CSV export. Samples carry an orange banner, are never written to Apple Health, and never appear in exported files.

Rep counts are too high or too low

Counts are estimated from wrist acceleration peaks. Adjust that activity's sensitivity and minimum rep interval in Settings — if it over-counts, raise the interval first; if it under-counts, lower it. Strap fit and wear position (one finger above the wrist bone) matter a lot. Demo Mode is the quickest way to compare settings.

A session was interrupted

The app saves a checkpoint every five seconds. Reopening after an interruption resumes from the last checkpoint. This is not lossless — the final few seconds before the interruption may be missing.

Recorded on Watch, but nothing on iPhone

Check that Bluetooth is available on both devices and the iPhone app is in the foreground. The Watch sends a status snapshot once per second, and saved session files queue for sync after reconnection — no need to record again. Control requests (start, pause, stop) are not queued, so anything tapped while disconnected has to be redone.

Duplicate workouts in Apple Health

Turn off "Automatic sync to Apple Health" in the Overview tab and sync manually from each session's detail screen instead. Duplicates already written must be deleted in the Health app — OddRep cannot remove them.

Does any data leave my device?

No. The app contains no networking code. Sessions live only on your Watch and iPhone. See the privacy policy.
