OddRep 1.0 — demo recording

A 31-second iPhone screen recording captured on a real device: the app connects to a paired Apple Watch, runs a live wrist-motion session with phone-side start / pause / finish, then shows the saved record, trends and insights.

31 s · iPhone screen recording Real Apple Watch paired English UI No account required

What the recording shows

  • 0:00
    Launch from the Home Screen The OddRep icon opens straight into the Workout tab. No sign-in, onboarding wall or permission prompt stands between launch and first use.
  • 0:03
    Phone → Watch handshake Status moves from “Checking Watch…” to “Waiting for Watch…”, then to an active session. A start request sent from the phone is executed by the Watch once it is reachable and idle; requests are never queued for later.
  • 0:12
    Live session, mirrored state The Watch is recording “Fire drilling” (a built-in rep-counting activity). Recorded time, total reps, reps per minute, heart rate and counting time refresh from 1 Hz snapshots. An iPhone snapshot timestamp is shown so the reviewer can see data freshness.
  • 0:19
    Finishing from the phone Pause and Finish are available on both ends. Tapping Finish asks for confirmation because the save happens on the Watch; the iPhone keeps the last snapshot until the Watch reports that the session was saved.
  • 0:23
    Overview: trends Total duration and record count for the selected range, a daily-duration line chart and a daily-reps bar chart. Demo/sample records are excluded from these totals and labelled as such.
  • 0:27
    Records and Insights The record saved from the Watch appears in Records with reps, duration and average cadence, and is exportable to CSV. Insights aggregates cadence and heart rate across sessions only where measurements exist; missing values are never estimated.

Good to know

The Watch screen is not captured

The Apple Watch cannot be captured by an iPhone screen recording. Watch-side actions are visible indirectly, through the mirrored session state on the phone. A desk-based Watch interface check is available in-app: Settings › Data › Demo Mode.

Test data, real sensors

Sessions in this recording come from wrist motion on a real Watch. Rep counts are estimates derived from wrist acceleration peaks — not activity recognition, form evaluation or medical measurement.

No account, no backend

Nothing is uploaded to the developer. There are no analytics or advertising SDKs. Watch↔iPhone transfer uses WatchConnectivity; Apple Health sync is optional and can be turned off in the app.

Want to try it without a Watch?

Open Records › Sample Experience › View Sample Records to inspect four sample sessions with charts. Samples are clearly marked, are not written to Apple Health and cannot be exported.
